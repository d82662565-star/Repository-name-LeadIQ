\# LeadIQ



LeadIQ is a sales intelligence and lead qualification application designed to help sales teams identify, prioritise and manage high-potential business leads.



\## Problem



Sales teams often manage a large number of leads but have limited time and resources to follow up with every prospect equally.



LeadIQ helps solve this problem by combining lead qualification, scoring, sales intelligence and pipeline analysis in one application.



\## Key Features



\- Lead scoring from 0–100

\- High, Medium and Low priority classification

\- Lead database management

\- Search, filtering and sorting

\- Sales pipeline tracking

\- AI-style rule-based lead intelligence

\- Qualification gap identification

\- Potential objection identification

\- Next-best-action recommendations

\- Follow-up priority recommendations

\- Sales opportunity classification

\- Revenue potential analysis

\- Industry-level analytics

\- Lead performance dashboards

\- CSV and Excel export
